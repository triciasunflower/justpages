Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Just Pages Studio handles information when you use this website and its printable generators.

1. Overview

Just Pages Studio is designed to help visitors create printable planners, worksheets, puzzles, and spreadsheet templates. We aim to keep the website simple and privacy-conscious.

This policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices you have when using the website.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to enter information into the generators, such as names, titles, dates, worksheet settings, puzzle words, planner details, or spreadsheet preferences. This information is used to create the printable page, PDF, puzzle, worksheet, or spreadsheet you request.

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, subject, and message. This information would only be used to respond to your message.

3. Generator content

The content you type into a generator is generally used to create your output in your browser. Some features may store temporary settings in your browser to improve the experience, such as remembering selected options or keeping a preview available while you are on the page.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into printable templates unless you are comfortable including it in the file or page you create.

4. Automatically collected information

Like many websites, Just Pages Studio may automatically receive basic technical information, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location based on IP address, referring page, and general usage activity.

This information may be collected through hosting logs, analytics tools, security tools, or similar technologies. It helps us understand website performance, fix errors, improve pages, and protect the site from misuse.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser technologies to support basic site functionality, remember preferences, improve performance, or understand general usage patterns.

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies or local storage, some website features may not work as expected.

6. Analytics and third-party services

We may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, form handling, security, fonts, or website tools. These providers may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

If advertising, analytics, or embedded tools are added in the future, this policy may be updated to explain those services more clearly.

7. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and improve the printable generators.
  • Create previews, downloads, PDFs, worksheets, puzzles, or spreadsheet files.
  • Respond to messages submitted through the contact form.
  • Understand how visitors use the website.
  • Fix bugs, improve performance, and maintain website security.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, or unauthorized activity.

8. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the website, such as with hosting providers, analytics providers, form-processing services, security tools, or when required by law.

9. Data retention

Information entered into browser-based generators may remain only in your browser unless a feature specifically sends or stores it elsewhere. Contact form submissions, server logs, analytics records, or security logs may be retained for a reasonable period as needed for communication, troubleshooting, legal, or security purposes.

10. Children’s privacy

Just Pages Studio may include learning-related printable tools, but the website is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted personal information, they may contact us and request deletion.

11. Your choices

You can choose not to submit information through forms or generator fields. You can also clear cookies, local storage, and browser history through your browser settings.

If you contact us, you may request that we delete or update personal information you previously provided, where applicable.

12. Security

We use reasonable efforts to keep the website secure. However, no website, hosting platform, form tool, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Links to other websites

This website may include links to other websites or tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Contact page to send a message.
